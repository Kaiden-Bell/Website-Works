1. Universal & Operational (Asked by EVERY Visitor)

Q: "What geographic areas do you serve, and do you travel?" (Reno, Sparks, Carson City, Lake Tahoe, and outer travel).

A: Local to Reno, Sparks and travel to Carson City. No outer travel.

Q: "How far in advance should we book?" (6–12 months for weddings/corporate galas; 2–4 weeks for standalone balloon decor or concessions).

A: A client may book as far in advance as needed, depends on the event. Though we tend to avoid accepting events that are too close (e.g. you have a wedding in a week and need a full day-of + setup). While realistically it shouldn't happen.

Q: "Can we hire you for just ONE service (like balloons or concessions) without full event planning?" (Crucial for casual party planners to know services are available à la carte).

A: Yes! We rent out concessions and do balloon arangements for events without full planning. See the pricing estimate or book a consultation for more information.

Q: "How does the initial consultation work, and is there any fee?" (Removes friction by confirming the consultation is free).

A: No inital fee for a consulation. It usually consists of a phone call, or online meeting via zoom to discuss your event, we establish a plan, build out a quote typically through intuit quickbooks your expected to pay prior to the event deadline with your payment method of choice.

Q: What happens if we need to cancel or move our date?

A: No return on deposit, however if you need to move the date the deposit will still go toward the event.


2. Pricing & Financial Clarity

Q: "What is required to lock in our date, and how are payments structured?" (Deposit/retainer % and payment schedule).

A: At least 25% up front as a inital deposit, oppourtunities to pay overtime, up till 2 weeks before the event, invoice is sent via Intuit Quickbooks 

Q: "Are delivery, setup, and teardown included in quotes?" (Assures users there are no surprise day-of fees).

A: Typically we do everything in house, from delivery, to setup to teardown, all of which is included in the main quote with no surprise fee.

"What does the $600 starting price cover?" (Clarifies what the baseline scoping fee includes).

While all events are specifically customized as a quote depending on amount of balloons, and other supplies, the average $600 base cost typically covers event delivery, setup, and teardown, with balloons included, thus a user isn't required to pay so many external costs for an arch aswell.

Q: Is there a travel charge for events outside Reno and Sparks?

A: For traveling outside of Reno and to Carson, there will be an extra charge for travel. This can and will be discussed over the consultation. 


3. Wedding-Specific Questions

Q: "What is the difference between Day-Of Coordination and Full-Service Planning?" (Helps couples self-select the right tier).

A: Day of Coordinator includes the following:
- Up to eight hours on-site with your lead coordinator and one assistant. Allowing you to enjoy every moment of your wedding day without any worry of the details
- Directing the ceremony processional
- Coordinating and assisting with the setup of the ceremony and reception
- Setup of wedding accessories such as guest book, place cards, favors, etc.
- Coordinating pinning of boutonnieres and ensuring VIPs recieve flowers
- Acting as the point of contact for all vendors
- Assisting the wedding party and guests as needed
- Distributing final payment and gratuity to vendors
- Dealing with any unforeseen hiccups

This package starts at $1500

The Full-Service Planning excludes the on-site coordinator duties, and the exclusion of POS for all vendors, and just involves setting up the wedding as if it was a different event.

Q: "Does your officiating service include custom ceremony writing and vow help?" (Confirms personalization).

Usually, we'll recite tradtional vows, and a prayer, but bride/groom may have different options in one of the many consultations.

Q: "Why do you have an in-house notary, and how does that help on our wedding week?" (Explains the unique perk of seamless marriage license verification).

A: As the Founder, Christa Bell has taken the initative to aquire a Notary license which allows her not only plan, and coordniate your wedding, but also allows seamless transition into a more legal side of things, ensuring your day is entirely taken care of without contacting several other vendors.

A: Is the notary service included, or billed separately?

Q: Notary as itself is a service, and so it will be billed separately, but usually there is no need for a notary at a wedding, just a witness required.

A: Yes, we can provide a COI and a W-9 if need be.

Q: "What event sizes and guest capacities can you handle?" (Validates scale capability for 250+ attendee company events).

A: We can handle every size. We've had events with more than 200+ people in attendance before, what matters to our team most is how much time we have to setup an event.

5. Decor & Rental Questions

Q: "How long do organic balloon installations last, and can they be set up outdoors?" (Durability, heat, and wind expectations).

A: Typically balloon installations indoors last the entire event. There have been issues in the past with installations outdoors and dealing with windy conditions. Typically if the setup is outdoors we cannot gurantee the installation will stay as is through the entire event (from setup to teardown) as its just the natural physics of the balloons.

Q: "Do concession machine rentals come with supplies and staff, or are they self-serve?" (Clarifies operational effort on event day).

A: Supplies yes, if purchased at $125/day, there's enough supplies for 25 people, additional supplies may be bought if requested at $5/additional-person. Self-serve up front, need to request an attendant at $10/hr ontop of the $125/day cost.

Q: What do the concession machines need on site?

A: Electricity, we'll provide everything else. We have a generator for events in more outdoor/rural locations, but that is an extra cost to be discussed through a consulation.

Q: What happens if weather turns on an outdoor event?

A: There have been times where we we're able to vacate outdoors and move into some indoor venue, but this does not ensure rebuilding the venue indoors. Materials may most likely end up damaged which won't allow us to toss everything inside mid way through an event. We most likely can save things like balloons and of course any of the consession rentals.

Q: What happens if rented equipment is damaged or goes missing?

A: Each client signs a contract saying they are liable for any damaged to rented equipment. Damages will be handled accordingly.
