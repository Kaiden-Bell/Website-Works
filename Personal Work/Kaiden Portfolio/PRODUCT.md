# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Begin with vanilla HTML / CSS / JS. Migrate to Vite + TypeScript once a design direction is confirmed. Deploy target is undecided.

## Users

Recruiters, potential freelance / contract clients, and professional peers — a general professional audience evaluating Kaiden's skills, work quality, and fit.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that showcases Kaiden Bell's creative and technical work across web development, full-stack software engineering, and UI/UX design. Success means a visitor quickly understands who Kaiden is, what he builds, and how to reach him — and leaves with enough confidence to start a conversation.

## Positioning

A multi-disciplinary developer-designer who works across the full stack and owns both the engineering and the interface. The portfolio itself is evidence of that range.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from job boards, LinkedIn, GitHub, or direct referral. They may scan on a phone between meetings or review on a desktop. Most visits are brief and evaluative — the site must establish credibility in the first viewport.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Covers three disciplines: web development, full-stack engineering, UI/UX design.
- No existing brand, logo, color palette, typography, or content to preserve.
- No existing projects or case-study assets yet — these will be supplied later.
- Content sections (projects, about, contact, etc.) are not yet locked; future design work will propose them.

## Evidence on Hand

None at this stage. Project screenshots, case studies, and testimonials do not yet exist and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The site's craft is the first portfolio piece; every interaction should demonstrate skill.
2. **Respect the visitor's time.** Scannable, purposeful content — no filler, no lorem ipsum shipped as real copy.
3. **Range as a signal.** Design and engineering depth should both be visible without requiring deep navigation.
4. **Invite action.** Every page state should make it obvious how to reach Kaiden.
5. **Grow with the work.** The structure must accommodate new projects, disciplines, and content without redesign.
